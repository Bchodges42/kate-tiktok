# Sunrise on the Reaping edits: image credits and sources

All images are official promotional material for *The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping* (Lionsgate, 2026): film stills, character posters and teaser/arena posters. No editorial, stock or public-domain images ended up in the edits (see "Could not access" below). Every file in `images/sotr/` is the original download, uncropped; the crops live in each config.

**Where the files came from.** Full-resolution copies were downloaded from TMDB's image server (`image.tmdb.org/t/p/original/...`), which hosts the studio's released stills and posters, or from Bleeding Cool's Lionsgate press galleries where TMDB had no copy. TMDB does not caption stills, so **identities were checked against Lionsgate press captions** published by Bleeding Cool (credit lines like "Photo by Murray Close/Lionsgate"):

- https://bleedingcool.com/movies/the-hunger-games-sunrise-on-the-reaping-teaser-poster-images/ (geese still: "Whitney Peak as Lenore Dove Baird and Joseph Zada as Haymitch Abernathy"; reaping walk: "Joseph Zada as Haymitch Abernathy")
- https://bleedingcool.com/movies/the-hunger-games-sunrise-on-the-reaping-trailer-posters-images/ (tributes in white: "Iona Bell as Lou Lou, Ben Wang as Wyatt Callow, Joseph Zada as Haymitch Abernathy, and Mckenna Grace as Maysilee Donner"; reaping stage: "Jesse Plemons as Plutarch Heavensbee and Joseph Zada as Haymitch Abernathy")
- Character posters carry the actor and character name printed on the poster itself (for example "Joseph Zada is Haymitch"). The names and titles sit outside every crop.

Nobody was identified from their face. Stills without a caption are described by what they show; where costumes and setting match a captioned still, the table says "likely, unverified". None of the edits name anyone on screen (there is no text in any edit).

## Could not access

- **Wikimedia Commons** (public-domain and Creative Commons photos and recordings): reachable, but rate-limited this session's connection to roughly one request every few minutes, so I couldn't bring in atmosphere or texture images. I didn't try to get around the limit.
- **Unsplash**: blocks automated access behind a bot check.
- **people.com, ew.com, movieinsider.com** (more captioned Lionsgate stills): the environment service refused to add these hosts.
- **Music**: per your direction, no track was sourced. Each edit runs on a steady BPM with a plain beat guide, for a sound added in TikTok.

## Rejected after inspection

- Blue-sky cornucopia (TMDB `fqOpEbrVRsZEsN47wjlL5tLSuTA`): 4K file, but an upscaled, compressed trailer grab. Blocky at 1:1.
- Couple walking in the meadow (TMDB `xxHzApYmRt6gFp8Du4Ap3mszCUs`): waxy, upscaled faces at 1:1.
- Lenore looking up (TMDB `pZhB13YT3GP72zzx0OrWOOPQSa6`): the face is too large for any 9:16 slice.
- Golden-hour heads (TMDB `isb8EErxytcvczhObJHqWu8ht3K`): in a 9:16 slice one face sits under TikTok's caption area, or the other loses its eyes.
- Kiss and Lenore with the geese in the meadow: dropped from edit 2 in the second round so it reads as District 12 rather than the couple.
- Plutarch with the TV camera: the camera carries a small logo, which counts as text.
- Arena overhead with the "08" and "04" graphics: used only where the crop leaves the numbers out.
