# Engagement defaults and the post kit

Why this matters: TikTok shows each video to a few hundred people first and widens the audience only if they watch. This account's edits were averaging about 4% watched, with many viewers gone inside 2 seconds, so most videos stopped near 300 views. The fixes are the opening frame and the length. The full research is in the report "TikTok Growth Report: Mood Boards and Fan Edits", https://claude.ai/artifact/APaxyJvvPjm4YyLckMasp9.

## Defaults for every edit

| Choice | Default | Reason |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Length | 8 to 15 s. Up to 20 s only when the song's phrase needs it. | Watch share and replays both rise as length falls. The viral references this account studied ran 9.5 to 11 s. |
| Photos | 12 to 28, curated to one tight world. Not the whole board. | Leaving weak or off-palette photos out matters more than using every pin. |
| Time per photo | 0.2 to 0.6 s. | Slower reads as a slideshow; faster flickers. |
| First frame | The most recognisable or striking photo for the niche, whole and well filled, at 0.00 s. | It is the only frame most scrollers see. |
| First 2 seconds | The busiest stretch: at least 3 photos have appeared by 2.0 s. | Motion at the start signals an edit, not a still. |
| Holds | Nothing holds longer than about 1 s until the final slide. The final slide holds 1 to 2 beats. | A long hold mid-edit is where viewers leave. |
| Build | The strongest remaining photo lands on the biggest hit near the end. | A payoff raises the share who finish. |
| Loop | Ends on a bar line; last photo leads into the first. | Replays add watch time. |
| Text | None rendered into the edit. | The account's taste. The hook line is typed in TikTok. |
| Sound | The silent copy is posted with the sound added inside TikTok. | The video joins the sound's page, and baked-in songs can be muted. |

When the user asks for a longer edit, make it, say once that a longer edit lowers the watched share, and include an 8 to 15 s cut of the same design.

## Choosing what to make

- **Name the niche in searchable words.** "Haymitch Abernathy aesthetic" can be found; "pretty board 3" cannot.
- **Time fandom edits to the fandom.** The best window is from a week before a release to two weeks after. Check the date before planning and say so if a moment is close.
- **Offer a series.** "Each district as a mood board, part 1 of 12" gives viewers a reason to follow and to ask for the next one.
- **One world per video.** Mixing two aesthetics in one edit weakens both, unless the edit is an explicit "this or that".

## Post kit

Ship this with every edit, after the report.

**Captions, three options.** Lowercase and short. The searched phrase comes first, then the format, then one easy question.

```
haymitch abernathy aesthetic | sunrise on the reaping mood board. which district are you?
coastal winter aesthetic | pinterest mood board. beach house or cabin?
```

**Text hook, two options.** One line of 3 to 8 words for TikTok's own text tool, shown for the first 2 seconds, repeating the searched phrase. Examples: "the hunger games as a pinterest board", "coastal winter, in 12 photos". The user decides whether to use it; it is the cheapest test they can run.

**Hashtags, 3 to 5.** One broad tag for the fandom or aesthetic, one or two specific, one for the format (#moodboard, #edit, #pinterestaesthetic). No #fyp or #viral.

**Sound.** The exact sound if the edit was built on one. Otherwise the tempo, with half and double time, so a matching sound can be chosen in the app.

**Cover frame.** Name the photo to use as the cover: usually the first frame.

**Posting checklist.**

1. Upload the silent 1080p copy with high-quality upload switched on.
2. Add the sound in TikTok and check the first cut lands on the beat.
3. Add the text hook, if using one.
4. Paste the caption and hashtags.
5. Reply to every comment in the first hour.
6. At 48 hours, note average watch time, watched full video, views and new followers.

## Reading their analytics

If the user shares analytics or screenshots, compare against the video's length, not across videos of different lengths.

| Pattern | Likely cause | Next change |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Most viewers gone by 2 s | The first frame gave no reason to stay | New opening photo, busier first 2 s, or a text hook |
| Steady loss through the middle | Pace too even, photos too alike, or too long | Shorter cut, more contrast between neighbours |
| A dip at one moment | A weak photo or a hold there | Swap or shorten that step |
| Viewers stay to the end | Room for more | Try a slightly longer cut or a second pass |
| Views from Search growing | The caption and hook phrase are working | Keep the phrase pattern |

A single video near 300 views is a small sample. Judge a change on 4 or 5 edits.
